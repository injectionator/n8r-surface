# Claude guidance

Read and follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for repository instructions, including
issue, branch, draft PR and verification requirements. Follow more specific
AGENTS.md files when working in their directories.

For labeling, follow the shared [issue status policy](CONTRIBUTING.md#issue-status-labels)
and [PR labeling policy](CONTRIBUTING.md#pull-request-labels): keep Kanban status
labels on issues only; apply relevant topic labels to PRs and use GitHub's native
draft, review and merged/closed states for their progress. Maintain labels when
creating or updating an issue or PR; labels do not authorize the next action.
